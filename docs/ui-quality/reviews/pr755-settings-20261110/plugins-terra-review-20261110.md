## Observable pair differences and limits
- Light All: U uses filled blue Install buttons and smaller badges; V uses outlined buttons and larger status/type pills. U gives repeated actions more salience and fits more content; V gives states more prominence and the action less visual emphasis. Both show warning text for Computer.
- Dark Installed: V's status/type pills are visibly larger than U's; both show a single enabled plugin with outlined Disable and More actions. Empty space follows the visible single-result state, not evidence of a layout fault by itself.
- Dark lower All: U's filled blue Install controls are much more prominent; V's outlined controls quiet the repeated actions and enlarge status labels. V has taller rows; the bottom-anchored crops therefore expose different upper fragments. Both show a lighter Fleet row, but hover/capture state may differ and cannot establish intent.
