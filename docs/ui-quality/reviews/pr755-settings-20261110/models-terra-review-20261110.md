## Observed comparison and limits

R and S preserve essentially the same overall hierarchy, surface treatment, controls and color assignments across matched themes. In the displayed dark overview and menu, R's provider row shows a right-pointing disclosure triangle, while S shows a downward triangle and its model row sits lower; the captures alone cannot establish why or whether this is a design change. The light images are visually very close at this scale. Both dark menu captures show a focused search field and checked, wrapped option; neither establishes the keyboard sequence. These observations do not compel a winner or a score change.

The Linear and Vercel images are enlarged documentation crops without full navigation, and T3 is a historical PR illustration with different task, crops and scale. They are not equivalent full-product captures. The candidate images are 1188×848 Linux Electron captures using isolated synthetic local FakeModel data. Their geometry/scenes are matched, but identity, incidental sidebar/history and capture moment differ, so this is not a perfectly controlled causal A/B. Static pixels do not prove provider save, persistence, network/privacy behavior, installation, keyboard sequence, screen-reader coverage, WCAG compliance or whole-product quality. Assignment model labels are not independent serving-backend attestation.
