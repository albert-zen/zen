## Comparison and limitations

R and S appear extremely close in hierarchy, placement, controls, color and surface treatment in these matched scenes. Both dark versions visibly show the model disclosure expanded and the single model row below it. The dark menu examples show an outlined search input and checked wrapped option. I do not see a reliable difference that warrants choosing a winner or changing a dimension rating. Small visual differences cannot confidently be attributed to a design change given the remaining session and capture differences.

Linear and Vercel are enlarged publisher documentation crops without complete navigation; T3 is a historical PR illustration with a different task, crop and scale. These are not equivalent full-product captures. Candidate images are 1188×848 Linux Electron captures with synthetic local FakeModel data; scene geometry is matched, but identity, incidental sidebar/history and capture moment differ. The disclosure correction matches one state, not all variables. Static images do not establish save or selection persistence, network/privacy behavior, installation, keyboard sequence, screen-reader support, WCAG compliance or whole-product quality. Model labels are assignment labels, not independent serving-backend attestation.
